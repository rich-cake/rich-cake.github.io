About

I am a PhD student at Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology), advised by Hiroshi Sasaki.

My research interests lie in computer architecture and processor design. My recent work explores hardware acceleration for detecting Spectre gadgets through parallel wrong-path execution.

Previously, I worked as an assistant engineer at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2025–2026), where I also completed my master’s in Computer Architecture under Yungang Bao. I received my bachelor’s in Electronic Information Engineering from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.
